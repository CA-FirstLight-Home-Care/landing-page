FirstLight Home Care: Southern California

Good care starts
with your local team.

We are the Southern California Alliance of FirstLight Home Care franchise owners. Each office is locally owned and operated by people who live and work in the communities they serve.


We provide compassionate, non-medical home care for older adults, people recovering from illness or surgery, and families seeking dependable support. From companion visits and personal care to dementia support and family caregiver respite, our local teams help people live safely, comfortably and independently at home.
