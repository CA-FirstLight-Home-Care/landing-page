## Social verification limits

- Only thirteen accounts are included: six Instagram, three X and four LinkedIn. All thirteen URLs are listed in the table; other platforms are not shown.
- Eight offices have no confirmed account on these three platforms. Their directory/contact treatment is identical, with no invented handles or links.
- National brand accounts were not mislabeled as local office accounts. Destination platforms sometimes block public fetches; official local outbound links establish office attribution where noted.
- A possible Rancho Cucamonga X account, @FirstLight_RCH, only links an old regional subdomain and was omitted pending stronger current corroboration.
- The available research used exact official public office pages, the owner-supplied Montclair hub and connected public X search. This is a verified subset, not an exhaustive platform-wide audit. Broader CLI web search was unavailable.
