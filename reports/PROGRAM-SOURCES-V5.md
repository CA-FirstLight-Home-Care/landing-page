## Boundaries
- No person is labeled qualified, eligible, approved or entitled to funding.
- No care rate, benefit amount, loan amount, approval speed, interest rate or financial eligibility threshold was added.
- No recommendation to borrow, sell a home, change assets, or surrender/sell insurance.
- No implication that dementia or Veteran status automatically unlocks a benefit.
- Free consultation/guidance does not mean care, loan products or paid filing services are free.
- Exact options, local office participation, insurer rules and program eligibility remain for the responsible office/program/professional to verify.
- ElderLife public home/product pages returned 403 to public retrieval; no facts were inferred from those failed requests. The opened co-branded Drive brochure and FirstLight partnership material supplied the financial facts used.
- The broad web-search CLI is not installed. No substitute search provider was used; research used the connected Drive search and direct readings of known official public sources. `/parallel-setup` would enable that optional research CLI for future broad searches.
