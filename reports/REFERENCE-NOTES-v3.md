## Pricing wording and why

Alliance-wide FAQ:

> Rates vary by local office, the help needed and the schedule. Care is generally billed by the hour, and a minimum visit length may apply. Ask your local team for a written quote after a free care consultation, including any holiday, overtime, mileage and cancellation charges. There is no single Southern California alliance rate.

No numeric office-specific rate is now published. Gary’s later steering requested equal treatment for all offices. D4 supports Montclair starting at $42/hour, a $42–49 range and a short-respite exception, but there is no supported alliance-wide numeric rate. Omitting this local example avoids both featuring Montclair and implying that any other office uses its pricing. The initial care consultation is free; subsequent assessment/visit charges must be confirmed locally, not universally waived.
